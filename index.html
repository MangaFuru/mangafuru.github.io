MangaFuru

MangaFuru 是一個漫畫閱讀器，不是線上漫畫商店。App 不會預先附漫畫；你可以匯入自己的 EPUB、PDF、CBZ，或連接自己架設的 Komga 書庫，再用書庫、清單與續讀整理閱讀進度。 MangaFuru is a manga reader, not an online manga store. It comes with no bundled catalog. Import your EPUB, PDF, and CBZ files or connect a Komga library you host, then organize everything with Library, lists, and resume.

定位很清楚Where we stand

MangaFuru 是什麼——以及不是什麼。 What MangaFuru is — and what it is not.

MangaFuru 用來閱讀你自己加入的內容。它不提供或代管漫畫，也不附官方內容目錄；檔案、連線方式與存取權限都由你自行選擇與管理。 MangaFuru reads content you add yourself. It does not provide or host manga, and it ships with no official catalog; your files, connections, and access rights remain yours to choose and manage.

  • 讀你擁有的書庫：你自架的 Komga、本機檔案，以及你自行設定的來源——全部由你掌握。Reads the library you own: your self-hosted Komga, local files, and the sources you configure — all in your hands.
  • 不代管內容：不上傳、不販售、不散佈漫畫，也沒有官方內容目錄。Hosts no content: no uploading, selling, or distributing manga, and no official catalog.
  • 本機優先：書庫、進度、清單與偏好主要保存在你的裝置上。Local-first: your library, progress, lists, and preferences live mainly on your device.
  • 不推薦來源：不提供來源清單或內容索引，也不協助繞過登入或付費。Recommends no sources: no source lists or content indexes, and no help bypassing logins or paywalls.
  • 免註冊：沒有帳號系統、不用登入，打開就能開始整理你的書庫。No account needed: there's no sign-up or login — open the app and start organizing your library.
  • 不取代你的判斷：你需確認自己有權存取與使用相關內容。Doesn't replace your judgment: you confirm you have the right to access and use the content.
