2. 我們的核心原則：本機優先、無帳號2. Our Core Principle: Local-First, No Account

MangaFuru 的根本設計原則是本機優先。實務上，這代表：

  • 沒有 MangaFuru 帳號系統。使用 App 不需註冊 MangaFuru 帳號；個別來源、Komga 伺服器或 Apple 服務可能需要各自的帳號或登入。
  • 您的書庫、閱讀進度與來源設定主要儲存在裝置上。第 3 節說明備份與匯出，第 4 節說明可能傳送至 Google 的分析與診斷資料；「本機優先」不代表所有資料都不會離開裝置。
  • 我們不託管、上傳、銷售或散布漫畫頁面，也不提供官方內容目錄或推薦來源清單。第 4 節另述的內部熱門度統計僅保留符合資格公開來源的受限名稱與雜湊內容鍵，不保存漫畫頁面或原始網址。

MangaFuru 是一款漫畫閱讀器，外加一個來源設定框架。應用程式僅在您的指示與設定下運作，向您所選擇的來源請求並顯示內容；所有的來源、檔案、帳戶、憑證與存取權限，都是由您自行設定與控制。

The fundamental design principle of MangaFuru is local-first. In practice, this means:

  • There is no MangaFuru account system. No MangaFuru registration is required; individual sources, Komga servers, or Apple services may require their own accounts or sign-in.
  • Your library, reading progress, and source configuration are primarily stored on your device. Section 3 covers backups and exports, and Section 4 covers analytics and diagnostics that may be sent to Google. Local-first does not mean that no data ever leaves your device.
  • We do not host, upload, sell, or distribute manga pages and provide no official content catalog or recommended-source list. The internal popularity measurement described in Section 4 retains only a bounded title and hashed content key for eligible public sources, never manga pages or raw URLs.

MangaFuru is a manga reader plus a source-configuration framework. Acting solely on your instruction and configuration, the app requests and displays content from the sources you choose; all sources, files, accounts, credentials, and access rights are configured and controlled by you.

3. 儲存在您裝置上的資料3. Data Stored on Your Device

下列資料主要在您的裝置上建立並儲存，不會整批傳送給 MangaFuru 團隊。您啟用的備份、匯出，以及第 4 節的分析與診斷可能涉及裝置以外的儲存或處理：

  • 您的書庫，以及匯入的內容項目；
  • 稍後再看／歷史清單與閱讀紀錄；
  • 閱讀進度（包含章節與頁面位置）；
  • 已安裝的來源紀錄、儲存庫設定與個別來源設定；
  • 應用程式偏好設定（例如語言、外觀、閱讀方向、快取設定）；
  • 封面、頁面影像與中繼資料的快取；
  • 本機診斷紀錄（log）。

您可在 App 內刪除個別項目或清除快取。刪除 App 不會一併刪除已匯出的檔案、iCloud 備份、已送出的分析資料或第三方來源所持有的資料；這些資料需依其儲存位置分別管理。

本應用程式內建一個 Local（本機）來源，作為一個內容中立的機制，供您匯入或閱讀您在自己裝置或網路上已經擁有並控制的檔案。此機制不提供漫畫目錄或推薦來源；App 可依您的操作建立本機書庫索引與快取。移除這個 Local 來源只會移除其進入點紀錄，而不會刪除您先前匯入的內容。

The following data is primarily created and stored on your device, not transmitted wholesale to the MangaFuru Team. Backups and exports you enable, and the analytics and diagnostics in Section 4, may involve storage or processing outside your device:

  • your library and imported content items;
  • read-later / history lists and reading history;
  • reading progress (including chapter and page position);
  • installed-source records, repository configuration, and individual source settings;
  • app preferences (such as language, appearance, reading direction, and cache settings);
  • caches of cover images, page images, and metadata;
  • local diagnostic logs.

You can delete individual items or clear caches in the app. Deleting the app does not also delete exported files, iCloud backups, previously transmitted analytics, or data held by third-party sources; manage those copies separately at their storage destinations.

The app includes a built-in Local source, which is a content-agnostic mechanism that lets you import or read files you already own and control on your own device or network. It provides no manga catalog or recommended sources; the app can build local library indexes and caches in response to your actions. Removing the Local source only removes its entry-point record and does not delete content you previously imported.

備份、iCloud 與匯出檔案Backups, iCloud, and Exported Files

若您啟用 iCloud 自動備份，App 會將備份檔儲存至您 Apple 帳號的 iCloud 空間，而非 MangaFuru 團隊的內容伺服器。依備份類別，檔案可包含書庫與分類、待讀清單、閱讀紀錄與進度、來源及儲存庫設定，以及可備份的 App 偏好設定；備份標頭亦可能包含裝置名稱、建立時間與 App 版本。Apple 依其 隱私權政策 處理 iCloud 資料。

App 原生備份不等同完整檔案備份：不包含下載的漫畫頁面影像或可重建的圖片快取，也不會備份 Cookie 或 Keychain 憑證。來源設定與內容中繼資料仍可能含有私人伺服器位址或個人資訊，請勿公開分享備份檔。關閉自動備份會停止後續自動備份，但不會刪除既有副本；請透過備份管理或 Apple 的 iCloud 儲存空間控制另行刪除。

手動匯出時，檔案會交給您在「檔案」或分享介面選擇的位置或服務；其留存與刪除取決於該位置。還原備份會將其中支援的資料寫回 App。請保管所需副本，並留意匯出、還原與刪除操作的提示。Apple 裝置層級的備份另依您的系統設定與 Apple 政策處理。

If you enable automatic iCloud backup, the app stores backup files in your Apple account's iCloud storage, not on a MangaFuru Team content server. Depending on the backup categories, files can contain your library and categories, read-later list, reading history and progress, source and repository configuration, and supported app preferences. Backup headers may also contain your device name, creation time, and app version. Apple processes iCloud data under its Privacy Policy.

Native app backups are not complete file backups: they exclude downloaded manga page images, rebuildable image caches, cookies, and Keychain credentials. Source configuration and content metadata may still include private server addresses or personal information; do not share backup files publicly. Turning automatic backup off stops future automatic backups but does not delete existing copies; delete those separately through backup management or Apple's iCloud storage controls.

Manual exports are handed to the location or service you choose in Files or the share sheet; retention and deletion depend on that destination. Restoring a backup writes supported data back into the app. Keep copies you need and review prompts for export, restore, and deletion. Apple's device-level backups are separately governed by your system settings and Apple's policies.
