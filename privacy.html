MangaFuru 隱私權政策MangaFuru Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026 年 8 月 5 日Last updated: August 5, 2026

MangaFuru 是一款以「本機優先」設計的 iOS 漫畫閱讀器，並非內容平台。我們不提供帳號系統，亦不託管或散布任何漫畫內容。您的完整書庫、閱讀進度、歷史紀錄、來源設定與快取仍留在裝置上；但應用程式預設開啟 Firebase 產品分析、當機與效能診斷，會傳送受限的功能互動、假名化安裝識別碼與診斷資料。符合資格的公開來源內容在安全開關啟用後，亦可能傳送來源代碼、不可逆內容鍵、公開漫畫名稱，以及閱讀或加入書庫訊號，用於內部熱門排行。您可以在設定中關閉分析；關閉後 Firebase Analytics、Crashlytics 與 Performance 的收集會停止。資料不會用於追蹤，亦不會分享給廣告 SDK。本政策亦涵蓋 GitHub Pages 網站與問題回報。

MangaFuru is a local-first iOS manga reader, not a content platform. We provide no account system and host or distribute no manga content. Your complete library, reading progress, history, source configuration, and caches stay on the device; however, Firebase product analytics, crash reporting, and performance diagnostics are enabled by default and transmit bounded feature interaction, pseudonymous installation identifiers, and diagnostic data. Once a separate safety switch is enabled, eligible public-source content may also send a source code, irreversible content key, public manga title, and read or library-add signal for internal popularity rankings. You can turn Analytics off in Settings; doing so stops Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance collection. The data is not used for tracking or shared with an advertising SDK. This policy also covers the GitHub Pages website and issue reporting.

2. 我們的核心原則：本機優先、無帳號2. Our Core Principle: Local-First, No Account

MangaFuru 的根本設計原則是本機優先。實務上，這代表：

  • 沒有 MangaFuru 帳號系統。您無需註冊、登入或建立任何帳號即可使用本應用程式。
  • 您的完整書庫、閱讀進度與來源設定留在裝置上；但第 4 節所列的受限互動事件與 Firebase 診斷資料可能傳送至 Google。公開來源內容的熱門度事件另受預設關閉的安全開關控制。
  • 我們不託管、上傳、銷售或散布漫畫頁面，也不提供官方內容目錄或推薦來源清單。第 4 節另述的內部熱門度統計僅保留符合資格公開來源的受限名稱與不可逆內容鍵，不保存漫畫頁面或原始網址。

MangaFuru 是一款漫畫閱讀器，外加一個來源設定框架。應用程式僅在您的指示與設定下運作，向您所選擇的來源請求並顯示內容；所有的來源、檔案、帳戶、憑證與存取權限，都是由您自行設定與控制。

The fundamental design principle of MangaFuru is local-first. In practice, this means:

  • There is no MangaFuru account system. You do not need to register, sign in, or create any account to use the app.
  • Your complete library, reading progress, and source configuration stay on the device; however, the bounded interaction events and Firebase diagnostics listed in Section 4 may be sent to Google. Public-source popularity events are additionally controlled by a safety switch that defaults to off.
  • We do not host, upload, sell, or distribute manga pages and provide no official content catalog or recommended-source list. The internal popularity measurement described in Section 4 retains only a bounded title and irreversible content key for eligible public sources, never manga pages or raw URLs.

MangaFuru is a manga reader plus a source-configuration framework. Acting solely on your instruction and configuration, the app requests and displays content from the sources you choose; all sources, files, accounts, credentials, and access rights are configured and controlled by you.

3. 儲存在您裝置上的資料3. Data Stored on Your Device

下列完整資料是在您裝置本機上建立並儲存，不會整批傳送給 MangaFuru 團隊。第 4 節所列的受限分類事件、符合資格的公開來源內容欄位與診斷中繼資料屬於例外：

  • 您的書庫，以及匯入的內容項目；
  • 稍後再看／歷史清單與閱讀紀錄；
  • 閱讀進度（包含章節與頁面位置）；
  • 已安裝的來源紀錄、儲存庫設定與個別來源設定；
  • 應用程式偏好設定（例如語言、外觀、閱讀方向、快取設定）；
  • 封面、頁面影像與中繼資料的快取；
  • 本機診斷紀錄（log）。

這些資料由 iOS 在您裝置上管理。您可以透過刪除個別項目、清除快取，或自您的裝置移除應用程式來刪除這些資料。

本應用程式內建一個 Local（本機）來源，作為一個內容中立的機制，供您匯入或閱讀您在自己裝置或網路上已經擁有並控制的檔案。它本身不隨附任何內容、不編列任何索引，亦非由我們提供或推薦的內容來源。移除這個 Local 來源只會移除其進入點紀錄，而不會刪除您先前匯入的內容。

The following complete datasets are created and stored locally on your device and are not transmitted wholesale to the MangaFuru Team. The bounded categorical events, eligible public-source content fields, and diagnostic metadata listed in Section 4 are exceptions:

  • your library and imported content items;
  • read-later / history lists and reading history;
  • reading progress (including chapter and page position);
  • installed-source records, repository configuration, and individual source settings;
  • app preferences (such as language, appearance, reading direction, and cache settings);
  • caches of cover images, page images, and metadata;
  • local diagnostic logs.

This data is managed by iOS on your device. You can delete it by removing individual items, clearing caches, or uninstalling the app from your device.

The app includes a built-in Local source, which is a content-agnostic mechanism that lets you import or read files you already own and control on your own device or network. It ships with no content of its own, indexes nothing, and is not a content source provided or recommended by us. Removing the Local source only removes its entry-point record and does not delete content you previously imported.

Analytics is enabled by default. MangaFuru uses Firebase Analytics to understand feature adoption, preference distribution, and the purchase funnel; Crashlytics and Performance to improve stability and performance; and Remote Config for safety switches and A/B tests. Under the Firebase SDK lifecycle, Crashlytics' persisted collection state becomes fully effective on the next App launch. Opting out does not delete data uploaded while collection was previously enabled. Remote Config may remain operational for safety and configuration, but it cannot override your choice to send product events.

MangaFuru lets you add "Repositories" and install or enable "Sources". A source may be a third-party extension, a remote service, or a local service running on your own network or device. Acting on your instruction and configuration, the app then browses, searches, opens, and displays content from those sources you configure.

Because some sources may run on your local network, or because the app itself may host a local server on your device, iOS may prompt you to grant Local Network permission. This permission is used to communicate with local source services on your device or local network, not to transmit your activity to us.

You are solely responsible for the legality and rights of whatever sources and content you configure and access, and for complying with applicable law and each source's own terms. The MangaFuru Team recommends no particular source and ships no curated source list; the built-in Local source described above is only a content-agnostic local mechanism and provides or points to no content.
